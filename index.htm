SECTION I: CORE ECONOMIC PRINCIPLES

1.Equal Standing & Acces

Following Article IV, Section VII of the Constitution, economic opportunities shall be accesible to very citizen regardless of birth, station or standing.

2.Right to Property

Article IV, Section III, every citizen has the right to acquire, hold and dispose of property without arbitary state seizure.

3.Regional Prosperity

Development shall extend across all Duchies, ensuring capital and resources local support commerce, infrastructure and regional provincial councils.

4.Fiscal Integrity

Public funds directed by the Council of State shall prioritize long-term economic stability, transparent acounting and growth.
