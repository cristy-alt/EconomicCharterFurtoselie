SECTION I: CORE ECONOMIC PRINCIPLES

1.Equal Standing & Acces

Following Article IV, Section VII of the Constitution, economic opportunities shall be accesible to very citizen regardless of birth, station or standing.

2.Right to Property

Article IV, Section III, every citizen has the right to acquire, hold and dispose of property without arbitary state seizure.

3.Regional Prosperity

Development shall extend across all Duchies, ensuring capital and resources local support commerce, infrastructure and regional provincial councils.

4.Fiscal Integrity

Public funds directed by the Council of State shall prioritize long-term economic stability, transparent acounting and growth.

SECTION II: CITIZEN RIGHTS & DUTIES

1.Rights

Right to Petition and Expression: Article IV, Section IV & V, every citizen has the right to express economic views and petition to the Ministries and the Ćiftom to adress economic complaints without fear of punishment.
Right to Privacy: From Article IV, Sextion X, every citizen holds the right to personal and financial privacy, protected against arbitrary or unnecessary survillance by the government.
Right to Fair Proceedings: Following Article IV, Section VI, every citizen accused of any regulation is entitled to fair notice and an impartial proceeding.

2.Duties

Duty of Honesty in Public Matters: from Article III, Part II, Section III, every citizen engaging in official registrations, petitions or commercial filings must do so honestly, without presenting falsehoods to state institutions.
Duty to Obey the Law: from Article III, Part II, Section II, every citizen must abide by the commercial laws and executives directives duly enacted by the Ministries and the Ćiftom.
Duty to Respect the Rights of Others: Article III, Part II, Section IV, citizens must exercise their economic rights without infringing upon the prosperity or property of fellow citizens.
